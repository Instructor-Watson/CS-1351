# Project Proposal Rubric — 100 points

| Criterion | Weight | Outstanding (100%) | Proficient (50%) | Developing (0%) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Purpose and Intended User | 10% | The proposal clearly states a concrete project title, explains the problem it addresses, and identifies the intended user audience with specific details. | The proposal includes a title and purpose, but one element of purpose or user may be underdeveloped or only broadly described. | Key purpose or the intended user is missing or unclear, making the project's direction hard to understand. |
| Part 1 Workflow | 20% | A complete Part 1 workflow is described from start to finish, including input, processing, and output, with feasible steps and milestones. | The workflow exists but contains gaps, overlaps, or includes tasks that are unrealistic for a single Part 1 iteration. | No workable Part 1 workflow is defined; the plan lacks a clear start, process, and finish, making execution unclear. |
| Foundational Java Feature Map | 25% | All required Java concepts (console I/O, variables, conditionals, relational/logical operators, loops, methods beyond `main`, and an array) are mapped to concrete, implemented features in the proposal. | Several Java concepts are identified, but at least one is vague or not clearly tied to a concrete feature. | Key Java concepts are missing or not adequately connected to the project's implementation plan. |
| Part 2 Plan | 20% | The Part 2 plan shows how string processing, loop-based input validation, and collection use will be strengthened, with concrete methods or classes suggested. | The Part 2 plan includes some useful ideas, but one of the three required areas (strings, validation, or collections) is vague or disconnected from the overall design. | Most or all Part 2 additions are absent or not integrated into the plan. |
| Examples, Files, and Timeline | 15% | Three concrete example inputs with their expected outputs are provided, and the required files and realistic timeline are clearly outlined. | Two items are complete (for example, examples or timeline), but at least one area is incomplete or unclear. | Planning details are largely absent; examples, files, or timeline are missing or not credible. |
| Risks and Fallback Scope | 10% | The proposal identifies at least two realistic risks and provides practical, smaller fallback plans for each. | Risks are noted, but fallback plans are weak, vague, or not actionable. | No meaningful risks or fallback strategies are described. |
